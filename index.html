Frequently Asked Questions

Is MathSprint free to play?

Yes! MathSprint is 100% free to play with all modes, difficulty levels, and achievements accessible. It is supported by non-intrusive advertisements.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Absolutely. All game logic, question generation, and score tracking happen entirely offline on your device.

How is the Brain Age score calculated?

Your mathematical brain age is calculated using a formula that weights arithmetic difficulty, response speed in milliseconds, and accuracy across a battery of test questions.
